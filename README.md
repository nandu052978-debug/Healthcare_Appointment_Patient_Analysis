# Healthcare_Appointment_Patient_Analysis
This project analyzes healthcare appointment operations, patient visit patterns, no-shows, cancellations, waiting time, satisfaction and modeled revenue using appointment-level synthetic data.
It demonstrates how operational analytics can support scheduling, capacity planning and patient-experience decisions.
Business Problem
A healthcare organization wants to understand:
How efficiently appointments are being completed
The no-show and cancellation rate
Which departments have the highest demand
Whether appointment type or booking lead time is associated with missed visits
Where waiting times are highest
How patient satisfaction varies
What operational actions could improve appointment utilization
Objectives
Analyze appointment volume and outcomes.
Measure completion, cancellation and no-show rates.
Identify demand patterns across departments and visit types.
Analyze no-show behavior by booking lead time.
Evaluate waiting time and satisfaction.
Analyze patient utilization and repeat visits.
Track modeled appointment revenue.
Build an executive-ready Excel dashboard.
Translate findings into operational recommendations.

Business Recommendations
For this synthetic dataset, the analysis supports considering:
Targeted appointment reminders for higher-risk appointment groups.
Reconfirmation closer to appointment dates for appointments booked far in advance.
Department-level capacity planning based on appointment demand.
Waiting-time investigation in departments with consistently longer queues.
Waitlist/backfill workflows for appointment slots released through cancellations.
Patient-experience monitoring using waiting time and satisfaction together.
These are analytical recommendations for the synthetic dataset, not clinical advice.
